Good to know

Questions girls and parents ask us

Where exactly is Siri Mane PG in Tumakuru?

5th Cross, Gangotri Road, S.I.T. Extension, Tumakuru 572103 — a residential lane within walking distance of Siddaganga Institute of Technology (SIT), and a short auto ride from Tumkur University, the bus stand and the railway station.

Is food included in the rent?

Yes. Breakfast, lunch and dinner are cooked fresh in our own kitchen every day — South Indian home food, with the week's menu shown in the resident app.

What rooms are available and what do they cost?

Furnished 2-sharing, 3-sharing and 4-sharing rooms with beds, mattresses, cupboards and storage. Monthly rent depends on the sharing you choose and includes food, WiFi, housekeeping and electricity. Call or WhatsApp 98802 17627 for today's availability and rates.

How safe is it?

Ladies only, with CCTV at the entrance and common areas, a secure gate, a resident warden, and visitor entries recorded in the app. Parents are welcome to visit before deciding.

Is it suitable for working women as well as students?

Yes. Our residents are students of SIT and Tumkur University and women working in Tumakuru's offices, hospitals and colleges. Reliable WiFi and a quiet study environment are part of the package.

Can I pay rent by UPI?

Yes — Google Pay, PhonePe or any UPI app, and cash. Residents see their dues and receipts in the Siri Mane resident app.
